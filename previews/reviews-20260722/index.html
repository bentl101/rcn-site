What the refresh addresses

The live pages are credible, but trust arrives too late.

The strongest opportunity is structural rather than cosmetic: reduce the early mobile workload, surface real customer evidence sooner, and make the route to a quote feel shorter.

01

Mobile header

The logo, wrapping phone number and menu compete for limited width.

02

First screen

03

Brand cards

Two narrow columns force awkward wrapping and make comparison harder.

04

Generic proof

05

Form distance

Visitors travel through a long page before reaching a lengthy single-step form.

06

Accessibility

Contrast, navigation state and reduced-motion handling deserve a refresh.

Recommendation

Use the carousel and the reviews page.

The reviews page should deepen trust, not carry it alone. On landing pages, keep one compact rating close to the hero and one context-rich carousel immediately before the main conversion point.
