Privacy notice

This page explains the intended handling of information submitted through the Discount Coach Tours enquiry form. It must be reconciled with the client’s formal privacy policy before public launch.

Information we collect

The enquiry form asks for contact details and travel preferences, including your name, email, phone number, destination, departure city, dates, trip length, number of travellers, budget, preferred operator, pace and optional notes. We may also record attribution and technical information needed to understand how the site is used and protect it from misuse.

How we use it

The information is intended to help Discount Coach Tours respond to your enquiry, compare relevant travel options, administer a future booking, improve its service and measure marketing performance. It is not intended to be sold to unrelated third parties.

Sharing and storage

Relevant information may be shared with travel suppliers when needed to prepare or manage your requested travel services. The production CRM, email routing, retention period and service providers are still to be confirmed with the client.

Your choices

You can ask about the personal information held about you or request a correction by contacting sales@discountcoachtours.ca. Marketing consent, deletion and formal privacy-contact wording must be confirmed before launch.
