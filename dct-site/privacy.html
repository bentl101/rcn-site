Privacy notice

This page explains how Discount Coach Tours handles information submitted through its enquiry form.

Information we collect

The enquiry form asks for contact details and travel preferences, including your name, email, phone number, destination, departure city, dates, trip length, number of travellers, budget, preferred operator, pace and optional notes. We may also record attribution and technical information needed to understand how the site is used and protect it from misuse.

How we use it

The information is intended to help Discount Coach Tours respond to your enquiry, compare relevant travel options, administer a future booking, improve its service and measure marketing performance. It is not intended to be sold to unrelated third parties.

Sharing and storage

Relevant information may be shared with travel suppliers when needed to prepare or manage your requested travel services. We use service providers to deliver and record enquiries securely and retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations.

Your choices

You can ask about the personal information held about you, request a correction or ask us to delete information where applicable by contacting sales@discountcoachtours.ca.
