Good to know

Coach tour questions

Does a coach tour mean every minute is scheduled?

No. The balance varies by operator and itinerary. Many tours combine included sightseeing with free afternoons or optional experiences. We can help find a pace that fits.

Can you help if I’m travelling solo?

Yes. We can look for solo-friendly dates, room options and itineraries. Single supplements and availability vary, so include this in your enquiry.

Are flights included?

It depends on the package. Tell us your departure city and we can explain the land-only and air-inclusive options available for your preferred tour.

Can you arrange a private group?

Group departures may be possible for families, clubs and community organizations. Select “Group enquiry” in the form and tell us roughly how many people are travelling.
